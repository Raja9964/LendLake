    Metric definitions

    Principal outstanding (POS)
    Disbursed amount minus principal repaid, on every open loan at month end.
    PAR 30+ and GNPA (90+)
    Share of POS on loans more than 30 or 90 days past due. DPD counts from the oldest instalment not fully paid by month end.
    Collection efficiency
    The KPI tile uses EMIs collected by month end over EMIs due that month. The leaderboard's overall figure adds arrears collected to the top and opening overdue to the bottom.
    Vintage, ever 30+
    Share of a cohort's loans that have been more than 30 days past due at least once by a given month on book.
